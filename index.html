About Me

Bruce Zhang received his M.S. degree in Computer Science from Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) and his B.S. degree in Software Engineering from Dalian Institute of Science and Technology. His research interests span AI Systems, Autonomous Driving, and Multimodal World Models, with broader interests in computer vision, deep learning, distributed systems, and high-performance computing. His previous work includes real-time computer vision systems, AI/pathfinding systems, and scalable distributed services. He is interested in developing efficient and intelligent systems that integrate multimodal perception, learning, and real-time computing.
